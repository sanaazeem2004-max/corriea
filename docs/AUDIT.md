## What exists
Utility bar (Serving San Diego · phone · social · Client Login) → nav (wordmark, Areas of Practice, About, Meet The Team, Book a Consult, Contact) → hero card with geometric pattern + CF monogram → six practice-area columns → "Connect with us" band → About → Meet the team → contact block → footer with full disclaimer → cookie modal (Accept / Settings / Reject).

Booking: `thecorreialawfirm.cliogrow.com/book`. Intake form: `…cliogrow.com/intake/78a22793…`. Client login: `clients.clio.com`.

## Problems
| Area | Issue |
|---|---|
| Hierarchy | The H1 is only the small label "Correia Law Firm"; the real headline is an H2. Three near-identical SEO-keyword H3s ("Estate Planning, Real Estate, Psychedelic Law, Business…") read as clutter. |
| Hero | Centered, paragraph-heavy hero inside a rounded card; the logo floats in an empty right half. No photography. Only CTA is "Contact"; booking is not in the hero. |
| Typography | One family (Lato) at similar weights throughout; long centered paragraphs (8+ lines) are hard to read. Nav links are underlined gold text with low contrast on white. |
| Practice areas | Six text columns of very different lengths (Psychedelic Law is ~3× the others), so the grid looks ragged. No images, tiny decorative icons, no per-service CTA. |
| Duplication | The intro paragraph appears three times (hero, About, attorney section). |
| Attorney | Small square photo centered below two long paragraphs; memberships buried in prose. |
| Contact | Four stacked, centered mini-blocks on dark brown; no contact form on page. |
| Spacing | Uneven vertical rhythm — large empty bands around "About" while practice-area columns are cramped. |
| Cookie consent | Bright default-blue buttons clash with the brand; modal covers ~half the hero on desktop and most of the mobile viewport. |
| Mobile (390) | Page becomes 10,000px tall; long centered text, wordmark wraps to two lines next to the Book button. |

## Keep
All six practice areas and their full copy, verified bio and four memberships, phone / email / address, Clio booking, intake and client-login links, Privacy Policy and Terms links, the full disclaimer, cookie choice (Accept / Settings / Reject), LinkedIn / Instagram / Enlightened Equire links.
